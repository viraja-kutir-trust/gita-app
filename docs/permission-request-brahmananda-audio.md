**Subject:** Request to use Swami Brahmananda's Bhagavad Gita chanting in a free, non-profit study app

Hari Om,

My name is Sai Teja Vadlapatla. For the past few years I have maintained **Gita All-In-One**, a free Bhagavad Gita study app on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store. It has no advertisements, no paid features and collects no personal data; it exists only to help students study the Gita.

The app presents each sloka with its transliteration, word-by-word meanings, and the translations and commentaries of the traditional ācāryas — including Śrī Śaṅkarācārya's Bhāṣya — and of modern teachers such as Swami Sivananda and Swami Gambhirananda. It also has a verse-by-verse "chanting mode" to help students learn the correct recitation.

For this, I would be deeply grateful for permission to use **Swami Brahmananda ji's "Geeta Chanting" recordings**, played verse by verse within the app. Swamiji's clear, traditional chanting is the finest guide to correct pronunciation that I know of, and it is also the recording used on the IIT Kanpur Gita Supersite.

What I would commit to:

- The recordings would be used only inside the free app, unaltered, and never sold or monetised in any form.
- Every verse that plays would credit "Chanting: Swami Brahmananda, Chinmaya Mission" (or any wording you prefer), with a link to the Chinmaya Mission store where the full album can be purchased.
- I would host the files myself, so there is no load on your servers, and remove them promptly if you ever ask.

If it is easier, I would also be glad to purchase the official MP3 release and use those files, split by verse, under the same conditions.

Thank you for considering this request. I would be happy to share a test build of the app or answer any questions.

With pranams,
Sai Teja Vadlapatla
