## Full description (4000)
```
Gita All-In-One is a free, ad-free companion for studying the Śrīmad Bhagavad Gītā — verse by verse, word by word, with the commentaries of the great teachers.

READ EVERY VERSE PROPERLY
All verses, laid out as they are meant to be read: two lines for anuṣṭubh verses, four for the longer triṣṭubh verses, the speaker shown separately and the verse number in your script's numerals. Each verse comes with its transliteration and your chosen translation. Swipe to move to the next verse.

UNDERSTAND EVERY WORD
Tap any word in a verse to see its meaning, its root, and its grammar — case, number, gender, tense — and step through the verse word by word. Word-by-word meanings from Swami Sivananda, Swami Swarupananda (1909) or Swami Mukundananda; grammar from the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit.

THE GREAT COMMENTARIES
The Sanskrit commentaries of Śrī Śaṅkarācārya, Rāmānujācārya, Madhvācārya, Śrīdhara Svāmī, Madhusūdana Sarasvatī, Vedānta Deśika, Abhinavagupta and others; Śaṅkara's Bhāṣya in English (A. Mahadeva Sastri); and the Hindi and English commentaries of Swami Ramsukhdas, Swami Chinmayananda and Swami Sivananda.
• Search inside the commentaries and jump straight to the passage
• Filter by school — Advaita, Viśiṣṭādvaita, Dvaita and more — and by language
• Pin the commentators you study with so they're always first
• Read Sanskrit commentaries in Devanagari, Telugu or IAST

COMPARE TRANSLATIONS
A dozen translations in English, Hindi, Telugu and Tamil — Swami Gambhirananda, Swami Sivananda, Swami Swarupananda, Shri Purohit Swami, Annie Besant and more — side by side. Trace one Sanskrit word across all of them.

HIGHLIGHT, NOTE AND KEEP
Select any words to highlight them in four colours, copy or share them, or add them to your notes. Bookmarks, notes, highlights, history and your progress through all 18 chapters live in the Library.

SEARCH THE WHOLE GITA
Verse numbers ("2.47"), Sanskrit in any script, romanised words with or without diacritics ("karmanye"), and English or Hindi phrases across every translation and commentary.

CHANTING MODE
Read a chapter — or the whole Gita — as one continuous stream of verses, with the current verse lit up and gentle auto-scroll.

READ YOUR WAY
• Sanskrit in Devanagari, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, Malayalam, Bengali, Gujarati or IAST
• Light, sepia and dark themes; adjustable text size
• Fully offline — everything is in the app
• No ads, no account, no tracking — free for everyone, always

Sources are credited in the app under Settings → Sources & credits.
```
